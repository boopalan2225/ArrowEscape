Privacy Policy Terms of Service

In Plain Words

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell anything about you. The game runs offline; the only data that leaves your device is what ads and the optional Google Play Games sign-in need.

What stays on your device

  • Current level, best level and stars, for each game mode.
  • Your preferences and settings: theme, sound and haptics.

This lives strictly in the app's private sandbox storage and is removed automatically when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Rewarded and interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, coarse location derived from your IP address, and basic device and app diagnostic information.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android Settings under Privacy → Ads. For more information, please review the Google AdMob Privacy & Technologies Policy.

Optional cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is stored in your own Play Games saved-games cloud space so it follows you to a new device. We see only your Play Games display name and that saved progress. You can sign out at any time in Settings; playing offline without an account is always fully supported.

Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and intentionally collects no personally identifiable information. Where required by applicable policies (such as Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA), ad requests are marked as child-directed so only non-personalised ads are served.

Security & Data Encryption

We care about protecting your information. Any transmission of data to third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Google Play Games) is conducted using industry-standard secure communication protocols (HTTPS/TLS encryption).

Your choices & Data Deletion

  • Reset local progress: Erase all saved progress at any time directly in the game's Settings screen.
  • Sign out of cloud save: Disconnect from Google Play Games in Settings to cease cloud syncing.
  • Uninstall: Erases all local game data, stars, levels, and settings permanently from your device.
  • Cloud save deletion: To request deletion of cloud game records associated with your Google Play Games account, contact us at the email below.

Third parties involved

  • Google AdMob – serves and measures ads (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Games – optional player sign-in and cloud game progress save.
  • Google Play Store – distributes the game and its application updates.

No other third party receives data from this game, and we operate no independent analytics or background tracking services of our own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with an updated date, and significant changes will be called out in the app's release notes.

Questions & Contact

Questions about this policy, or want assistance deleting your saved cloud progress? Write to support@arrowescape.app and we will respond within 30 days.
