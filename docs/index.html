In Plain Words

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell anything about you. The game runs offline; the only data that leaves your device is what ads and the optional Play Games sign-in need.

What stays on your device

  • Current level, best level and stars, for each mode.
  • Your settings: theme, sound and haptics.

This lives in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Rewarded and interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, coarse location derived from your IP address, and basic device and app information. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy, Ads.

Optional cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is stored in your own Play Games saved-games space so it follows you to a new device. We see only your Play Games display name and that saved progress. Sign out any time in Settings; playing offline is always an option.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal details. Where required, ad requests are marked as child-directed so only non-personalised ads are served.

Your choices

  • Reset progress any time from Settings.
  • Sign out of Play Games to stop cloud saves.
  • Uninstall to erase everything stored locally.

Who else is involved

  • Google AdMob – serves and measures the ads.
  • Google Play Games – optional sign-in and cloud save.
  • Google Play – distributes the app and its updates.

No other company receives data from this game, and we run no analytics or tracking of our own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a fresh date, and significant changes are called out in the release notes.

Questions & Contact

Questions, or want your cloud save deleted? Write to support@arrowescape.app and we will answer within 30 days.
